UX DIRECTION
This is a deliberate rebuild, not another control-shrinking pass.

- Product imagery is the visual center of every slot.
- Previous / Next are large, obvious edge controls and remain easy to use.
- Prices are removed from the Uniform builder.
- Headwear None is now a real compact ON/OFF toggle.
- Size selection remains accessible but visually subordinate to the product.
- Layer Top / Add Bottom remain discoverable secondary actions.
- Pick For Me stays visible without competing with the garments.
- The Add Look to Cart action remains the strongest conversion control.
- Multiple selected tops/bottoms display together within their category slot.

MOBILE
- The Look Studio is designed to fit inside the available phone viewport beneath the site header.
- Headwear, Tops and Bottoms share the available vertical canvas.
- Footer is hidden on this focused mobile experience.
- A fixed 56px Add Look to Cart bar stays at the bottom.
- Product prices are not shown.
- Controls and product images remain visible without requiring the user to scroll through the builder.

FUNCTIONS PRESERVED
- Shopify live products
- Live variants/sizes and sold-out states
- Pick For Me
- Headwear none
- Up to 3 tops
- Up to 2 bottoms
- Remove added layers
- One-request Add Look to Cart
- Existing Shopify cart drawer

No API, database, scope, or environment changes.
